# Spec: HD Atlas Daily Tip — Hebrew Morning Authority

## Problem

People know their Human Design type but say 'On the days I actually am able to follow my Strategy and Authority... I feel more aligned and notice way less bitterness. The problem is I keep forgetting.' They can name their Authority but don't run it against real daily decisions, and the apps they've tried are broken or only in English.

## Buyer

Hebrew-speaking Human Design fans in Israel, mostly in HumandesignIsrael Facebook group and Israeli HD WhatsApp groups, who know their type but don't apply Strategy and Authority day to day.

## Jobs

- Get one Hebrew reminder each morning that names today's Authority action in plain words
- Get one real decision to test the Authority on that day
- Not have to open an app or re-learn HD theory every morning

## In scope (v1)

- One-time WhatsApp intake form for birth date, time, place
- Daily 7am WhatsApp message: type + Strategy line + today's Authority tip + one decision prompt
- Manual Gumroad checkout, manual WhatsApp group add (v1 not self-serve automated billing gate)
- Hebrew only

## Out of scope

- Telegram bot (mentioned in offer but v1 ships WhatsApp only, Telegram is fallback if Twilio WhatsApp approval is slow)
- In-app chat/back-and-forth with the bot
- Compatibility/relationship readings
- English version

## Success metrics

| metric | target | milestone |
|---|---|---|
| landing page live with sample 3-day message | 1 page on Netlify with real Hebrew sample text | landing live |
| DMs sent to HD group members with sample | 10 conversations started, replies tracked in a sheet | 10 conversations |
| paid subscribers | 1 person paid ₪19 or ₪180 via Gumroad | first paying customer |
